Missed Deliveries & Returned Orders

Please ensure someone is at your specified shipping address to receive the medication. Mic Jeffon Pharmacy will not be responsible for replacements due to missed deliveries.

If your parcel is returned to us due to repeated failed delivery attempts, Mic Jeffon Pharmacy will issue no replacement or refund. Due to legislation regarding medication safety against the cold chain, any returned Saxenda pen cannot be resent and will have to be disposed of.

Please ensure you or someone else is available to receive the order on the day of delivery. If you would like your order shipped on a specific day, please let us know and we will be happy to dispatch it on another day.

Order Updates

If you have not received your order within 2 to 3 working days of placing it, please contact us immediately.

Courier companies do not open investigations into missing or undelivered parcels after 14 days of the order. If your order has not been delivered and you contacted us more than 14 days after placing the order, unfortunately we may not be able to investigate further.
